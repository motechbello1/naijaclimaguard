# NaijaClimaGuard / FloodPass product direction

Updated 26 September 2026. This is a launch hypothesis, not a claim of product market fit or forecast accuracy.

## Positioning

NaijaClimaGuard helps a person understand available flood context, report what is happening nearby and keep a checkable record. FloodPass is the evidence workflow inside that wider service. The public promise is **Know before. Act together. Prove after.**

The product must distinguish an official warning, weather context, a community observation and a checked record. A model score is not an official warning. A checked record is not independent field confirmation or a guarantee of payment.

## Visual identity

- Deep field teal `#0d2c2f`: sober, legible foundation.
- Pale mineral ground `#f3f4ed`: calm reading surface.
- Signal lime `#daf18c`: focused calls to action, never the sole carrier of danger.
- Instrument Serif for human editorial emphasis; Manrope for interface text; DM Mono for sources, labels and data.
- Documentary imagery of real places, with attribution and no fabricated testimonial. Use illustration only when it explains data.
- Motion indicates sequence or response: restrained entrance, hover and state transition. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

The reference study was about craft, not copying layouts: [Pentagram's CTrees identity](https://www.pentagram.com/work/ctrees), [Linear's design refresh](https://linear.app/now/behind-the-latest-design-refresh), [Google Flood Hub's public and expert framing](https://blog.google/intl/en-africa/company-news/advanced-flood-hub-features-for-aid-organizations-and-governments/), and [W3C reduced-motion guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/Understanding/animation-from-interactions.html). A high budget site is a process of research, original art direction, content, engineering, accessibility and field testing; a CSS pass alone cannot establish an equivalent market value.

## Who pays, and why

| Customer | Job | Offer | Readiness |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Person facing flood risk | Understand available context, report water, check a code | Public web access | Open, subject to live data availability |
| Response organisation, lender, insurer | Review a record with source, status, checks and access log | Scoped pilot; setup/service fee and agreed usage terms | Proposal; no signed partners claimed |
| Family | Keep track of several chosen places and share check-ins | Family Watch, planned price ₦500/month | Waitlist; channel delivery not proven |
| Person considering rent or land | Review known local history before a decision | One place report | In development; checkout gated |

The immediate commercial experiment is a paid institutional pilot in one geography and one decision workflow. A proposal should name the buyer, permission to access evidence, pilot duration, service level, a fee, and a decision rule for continuing. A pilot must not sell private report data without consent and a legitimate use. Keep official warnings and reporting available to everyone.

## Validation gates

1. **Public safety:** verify source and timestamp, failure state, official warning precedence, accessible language, low bandwidth performance and mobile usability with people in a target community.
2. **Record quality:** measure reports received, geolocation quality, duplicate handling, independently confirmed cases, erroneous records, corrections and revocations. Do not call a software score field verification.
3. **Institutional value:** measure median time to triage, fraction of records usable in a real review, false positives, field visits avoided or better directed, and the buyer's willingness to pay. Record baseline first.
4. **Family delivery:** prove opted-in contacts, message and call delivery under poor connectivity, clear consent, cancellation, support and a refund path before enabling checkout.
5. **Unit economics:** calculate actual data, messaging, voice, storage, review, support and payment costs per active household and per partner check. Price only after measuring usage and contribution margin.

## Repeat use with agency

The useful loop is: check a place when weather or a journey makes it relevant; receive a concise, sourced change when there is one; take an action; leave. Saved places and a short daily brief can reduce effort, but should have quiet hours, frequency control and an easy opt out. Do not use streaks, infinite feeds, vague danger badges or fabricated scarcity to create anxiety. Retention should track completed decisions and trusted reports, alongside opt outs and erroneous alerts, rather than screen time.

## Present limits

The rainfall index is a heuristic using Open-Meteo context, not a validated street-level flood forecast. National LGA centre coverage counts a scan network, not local prediction accuracy. The live public record may be empty. WhatsApp, SMS, USSD and calls require operating integrations; the existence of a route or planned entitlement does not prove delivery. Never advertise assistance, cash cover, or a partner until an agreement and operating process exist.
