# Investor and Competition Readiness Brief

## Positioning

NaijaClimaGuard should not pitch itself as "another flood map" or "another weather dashboard".

The strongest product position is a **Flood Decision Network** for Nigeria: a layer that combines predictive signals, official warnings, source confidence, people/assets at risk, role-specific action, last-mile delivery, acknowledgement and post-event evidence.

That is strategically different from competing only on raw hydrological forecast skill.

## What the market already has

### Nigeria Hydrological Services Agency (NiHSA)

NiHSA publishes the Annual Flood Outlook with coverage across all 36 states and the FCT and operates flood-monitoring/forecast products. NaijaClimaGuard should treat NiHSA as an authoritative national hydrological institution and potential data/integration partner, not imitate or misrepresent its mandate.

Reference:
https://nihsa.gov.ng/products/3

### National Emergency Management Agency (NEMA)

NEMA coordinates preparedness and response. In June 2026, NEMA reported the Vice President's direction to activate a national Fusion and Trigger Room for flood and climate early action. This confirms that the institutional direction is moving toward anticipatory action and coordinated decision workflows.

Reference:
https://nema.gov.ng/dg-nema-attends-aatf-meeting-as-vice-president-directs-activation-of-early-action-platform-for-flood-response/

### Google Flood Hub

Google Flood Hub provides AI riverine flood forecasts and inundation information, including Nigeria, with forecasts up to seven days ahead in covered areas. Google has also expanded expert features, basin views, APIs and virtual-gauge approaches.

References:
https://blog.google/intl/en-africa/products/explore-get-answers/helping-more-people-stay-safe-with-flood-forecasting/
https://blog.google/intl/en-africa/company-news/advanced-flood-hub-features-for-aid-organizations-and-governments/

## Where NaijaClimaGuard must be stronger

The goal is not to claim a global model is weaker without evidence. The goal is to own a product layer that is difficult for a general-purpose global platform to localise deeply.

NaijaClimaGuard's defensible differentiation should be:

1. **Nigeria-specific decision orchestration**
   - Household, farmer, business and agency action paths.
   - One hazard signal can produce different operational actions without changing the underlying risk score.

2. **Official-warning precedence**
   - Connected authoritative advisories remain above model output in the safety layer.
   - The platform assists agencies rather than impersonating them.

3. **Last-mile delivery designed for Nigerian realities**
   - email, SMS, WhatsApp and voice architecture;
   - local-language platform support;
   - low-bandwidth/offline emergency content;
   - delivery and acknowledgement evidence.

4. **Flood Digital Twin / Impact Graph**
   - model people, stock, livestock, medicine, critical facilities, suppliers, responders and other dependencies;
   - prioritise what must be protected first.

5. **Closed-loop evidence**
   - preserve signal, source state, action, acknowledgement and verified outcome in an auditable trail;
   - distinguish user assertion, system action and official evidence.

6. **Institutional integration layer**
   - ingest verified partner sources;
   - expose source freshness and degraded states;
   - support command queues, reports and pilot evidence.

7. **Simple-to-technical experience**
   - same underlying evidence can be rendered for a community user, an executive, an analyst or an engineer.

## The acquisition/embedding story

The strongest strategic narrative is not "we will beat Google at global hydrology."

It is:

> Global and national systems can generate excellent hazard information. NaijaClimaGuard turns those signals into locally relevant action, delivery, accountability and measurable outcomes across Nigerian users and institutions. That makes the platform valuable as a standalone national product and as an integration layer for agencies, banks, insurers, telcos, humanitarian organisations or larger technology platforms.

This creates several strategic outcomes:

- partner/integrate with NaijaClimaGuard;
- license specific decision, delivery or evidence components;
- white-label the platform;
- adopt NaijaClimaGuard as an institutional layer over existing forecast feeds;
- acquire the platform/team if owning the Nigerian last-mile decision network is more attractive than rebuilding it.

Acquisition is an upside scenario, not a claim or guaranteed exit.

## Current proof stack

### Already defensible

- live product deployed;
- canonical public risk engine used across product surfaces;
- Riverine Watch v1 frozen shadow candidate;
- 4/5 eligible historical onset events detected in Lokoja + Makurdi = 80% retrospective event detection;
- PR-AUC 0.1763;
- ROC-AUC 0.8371;
- alert-episode precision 26.7%;
- approximately 1.83 false alert episodes/location/year;
- source-age fail-safe behavior;
- role-aware Action OS;
- official-advisory precedence;
- evidence ledger and provenance controls;
- institutional pilot workflow;
- multilingual and accessibility layer;
- verified-channel architecture for SMS/WhatsApp/voice where providers are actually configured.

### Must remain clearly bounded

- 80% is not national accuracy;
- Riverine Watch is not yet prospectively validated for autonomous public warnings;
- partner feeds not connected must not be shown as live;
- unconfigured delivery channels must not be shown as delivered;
- national economic savings have not yet been measured.

## Number strategy

### Number we can use now

`80% retrospective event detection (4/5 eligible events) for Riverine Watch v1 in Lokoja and Makurdi.`

Always keep the denominator visible.

### Number we should build next

A **national retrospective event-detection benchmark** across benchmark-ready states using independent documented flood events and nationally consistent historical features.

Headline format:

`X/Y eligible documented flood events detected retrospectively across Z benchmark-ready jurisdictions.`

This is the fastest honest route to a broader number while prospective evidence continues.

### Number we should not invent

- national real-time accuracy;
- fixed 7/14-day national lead-time accuracy;
- percentage reduction in losses;
- number of lives saved;
- superiority to Google/NiHSA without a matched benchmark.

## Economic pitch

World Bank reporting estimates median direct economic damage from Nigeria's 2022 floods at about **US$6.68 billion**.

A transparent stress-case sensitivity shows:

- 0.5% avoidable loss = US$33.4m
- 1% = US$66.8m
- 2% = US$133.6m
- 5% = US$334m

These are opportunity scenarios, not realized savings.

## What an investor should understand in 30 seconds

> Flood information is not the same as flood action. Nigeria already has forecasts, advisories and global flood tools, but people and institutions still need a reliable way to turn different signals into the right action, through the right channel, for the right asset, and prove what happened afterward. NaijaClimaGuard is building that decision network. We already have a live platform and a frozen riverine shadow model with 80% retrospective event detection on its current five-event evidence set. We are expanding the evidence architecture nationally while prospective validation continues, and we are measuring the economic value through institutional pilots rather than inventing savings claims.

## Competition-room rule

Never win by exaggerating.

If another platform has stronger hydrological forecast evidence, acknowledge it and show why NaijaClimaGuard can consume or complement that feed while adding Nigerian decision, delivery and accountability layers. A buyer or government agency is more likely to trust a platform that understands its boundary than one that makes an indefensible superiority claim.
