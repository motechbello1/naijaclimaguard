# NaijaClimaGuard Product North Star V2

Status: FROZEN BEFORE FEATURE REDESIGN

This document is the product gate for the next NaijaClimaGuard build. New features are not accepted simply because they work. They must make the product clearly more useful, more habit-forming, more defensible and more valuable than the original submission.

## The selling point

**NaijaClimaGuard tells you what tomorrow could take from you before it gets the chance.**

The plain-language explanation is:

**It tells you what environmental danger is coming, when it could affect you, whether your area is at risk, what it could cost you in money, time or disruption, what you should protect, and what to do before it becomes your problem.**

Flood is the first proven hazard engine, not the limit of the product. The product architecture may support flood, extreme heat, severe storms, drought/dry stress and other hazards only when each hazard has a defensible data and validation path.

## What the original submission did well

The original submission had a very clear national problem: flood intelligence existed, but ordinary Nigerians often did not receive it in a form they could understand and act on. It combined citizen delivery with institutional value and had a strong climate/flood identity.

The new build must preserve that strength. It must not become a generic weather app, grocery app, map app or dashboard.

## What the original submission did not solve strongly enough

The original product did not create a strong enough reason for a normal person to return every day when there was no major flood event. It also did not make the personal economic consequence of weather and environmental risk obvious enough.

V2 must solve both problems.

## The daily habit loop

The main daily question is:

**What could tomorrow take from me, and what should I do before it happens?**

The default citizen experience is called **My Tomorrow**.

A useful daily answer can include:
- what is coming or building around the user;
- when it may affect the user;
- whether Home, Work, School, Shop, Family or a normal route is exposed;
- likely effect on Safety, Money and Time;
- what to protect or change before the risk window;
- whether the situation has improved or worsened since the previous check;
- what actually happened after the event;
- how much avoidable disruption or cost the user may have reduced when that estimate is supported by real exposure data.

A quiet day is still useful. The product can say that nothing serious is expected and no action is needed. It should never invent danger to create engagement.

## Habit-forming, not manipulative

The goal is frequent voluntary return because the answer changes and is useful. The product must not manufacture anxiety, hide information behind artificial countdowns, create fake urgency, or use misleading streaks.

The strongest retention mechanisms are:
- saved people, places and routes that the user genuinely cares about;
- meaningful changes since the last check;
- a personal history of warnings, outcomes and useful actions;
- proof of time, disruption or money saved where measurable;
- one-tap sharing of a useful location warning to family, friends or colleagues;
- notifications only when there is something materially useful to know.

## Why a normal user would eventually pay

The free product must be genuinely useful. Payment must unlock more protection, convenience or proof, not basic safety information.

Potential paid value must be tested before final pricing. Candidate premium value includes:
- more saved people, places, assets and routes;
- earlier or more frequent monitoring where the source supports it;
- advanced personal impact and cost planning;
- richer history and monthly protection/savings summaries;
- household or family protection groups;
- business-grade reports for users who operate shops, farms, fleets or small companies.

The product should make the value visible before asking for payment. The desired user reaction is: **This has already saved me more than it costs.**

## The data business

NaijaClimaGuard must not depend on selling people's private information.

The commercial data asset is the aggregated relationship between environmental conditions and what actually happens in Nigerian life and commerce.

Over time the platform should be able to measure, with privacy protection and clear consent boundaries:
- which roads and areas repeatedly become disrupted under specific conditions;
- how long disruption lasts;
- where transport time or availability changes;
- where businesses report closures or operational interruptions;
- where property or assets repeatedly experience loss;
- where demand patterns change around weather events;
- which warnings and preparations were associated with lower reported disruption;
- prediction versus observed outcome by place, time and hazard.

The product should eventually sell **impact intelligence**, not a copy of public rainfall data.

Possible commercial buyers include insurers, banks, logistics companies, retailers, property businesses, agriculture companies, infrastructure operators and public agencies. Each commercial product must use aggregated or permissioned data appropriate to the use case.

## The share loop

Sharing must be useful even if the recipient has never heard of NaijaClimaGuard.

Examples:
- Send Mum her warning
- Share this route risk
- Send this area update to the family group
- Share tomorrow's business disruption outlook

A shared link should open directly into the useful location or risk view. The recipient should immediately understand why the sender thought it was worth sharing.

## Original-build comparison gate

Every major feature, model, screen or revenue mechanism must be reviewed against the previous build before it is locked.

It receives one of three judgments:

**WORSE THAN ORIGINAL**
It weakens clarity, trust, usefulness, proof, climate identity or commercial value. Reject or redesign it.

**UP TO PAR**
It preserves the original strength but does not create a clear improvement. It may support the product but cannot be a headline upgrade.

**CLEARLY BETTER**
It preserves the original strengths and adds a measurable improvement in at least one important dimension without weakening the others.

The dimensions are:
1. Citizen usefulness
2. Daily return reason
3. Warning/action clarity
4. AI/model evidence
5. Lead time and false-alarm burden
6. Personal economic value
7. Sharing and organic acquisition
8. Willingness to pay
9. Enterprise/data monetisation
10. Trust and evidence
11. Multi-hazard extensibility
12. Nigeria-specific usefulness

## Current comparison

### Original submission
Strengths: powerful flood problem statement, clear last-mile problem, citizen plus institution positioning, strong national relevance.

Weaknesses: episodic citizen use, weaker daily habit, weaker personal money/time consequence, narrower flood identity, less developed first-party impact-data business.

### V2 direction
Strengths: preserves early-warning purpose, expands to validated environmental hazards, adds when/what-to-protect/what-to-do, introduces My Tomorrow, creates a natural daily question, connects risk to Safety/Money/Time, supports sharing and builds an aggregated impact-intelligence asset.

Current judgment: **POTENTIALLY CLEARLY BETTER, NOT YET PROVEN.**

It becomes clearly better only when the AI evidence, daily experience, retention behaviour and willingness-to-pay assumptions are tested rather than merely described.

## Non-negotiable product test

Before final redesign is considered complete, a normal user should be able to explain the product to another person in one breath:

**NaijaClimaGuard tells me what tomorrow could do to me before it happens, when it will happen, what I should protect and what I should do.**

If the product becomes harder to explain than that, the redesign has drifted.
