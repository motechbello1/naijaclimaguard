# My Tomorrow: Original-Build Comparison Gate

Status: FROZEN PRODUCT ACCEPTANCE RULE

## North star

NaijaClimaGuard tells you what tomorrow could take from you before it gets the chance.

Every consumer-facing change must strengthen that promise. Working code, more screens or more data are not sufficient reasons to ship.

## Original build strengths we must preserve

The original NaijaClimaGuard had a clear Nigerian flood-warning story, official-warning context, risk checking, saved places, alerts, actions, reports, evidence/provenance, community reporting and institutional/commercial pathways.

The new experience is not allowed to weaken those capabilities. It must make them easier to understand and use.

## Why My Tomorrow must be clearly better

The original experience asks users to understand several product surfaces before they get the whole answer. My Tomorrow must give the ordinary user one immediate answer first, then reveal the underlying capabilities when needed.

The required answer order is:

1. What is coming or building around me?
2. When could it affect me?
3. What does it mean for my Safety, Money and Time?
4. What should I protect?
5. What should I do now?
6. What changed since my previous check?
7. Why does NaijaClimaGuard think this?

A user must not need to understand model names, API sources, dashboards or hazard terminology to get value.

## Daily-return gate

My Tomorrow must have useful output on both danger days and quiet days.

A quiet day is allowed to say clearly that nothing serious is developing. The app must not manufacture urgency to create engagement.

Daily return should become rational because the answer can change with location, saved people/places, routes, forecasts and new evidence.

Do not add meaningless streaks, artificial countdowns, fear prompts or manipulative notification loops.

## Sharing gate

Sharing must begin from a useful piece of intelligence, not an invite programme.

Examples include sending a family member their location warning, sharing a route disruption, or sending an area update. A recipient should see useful information before being required to create an account.

A share feature is not accepted merely because it generates a link. It must preserve location, valid time, hazard, action and evidence context.

## Payment gate

Safety-critical information remains free.

Paid consumer value must feel like additional protection or planning rather than removal of a safety paywall. Candidate paid value includes more protected people/places/assets, richer planning windows, history, change tracking, family coordination, advanced route/asset monitoring and validated economic exposure tools.

Do not ask for payment before the free experience has demonstrated concrete personal value.

No invented naira savings, fake discounts or fabricated premium urgency are permitted.

## Evidence gate

The screen must never turn an internal hazard score into a probability or confidence percentage unless that score has been calibrated and validated for that interpretation.

Economic amounts must be ranges with provenance when supported. If the data is insufficient, show the exposure or consequence without inventing a naira amount.

Official warnings retain precedence where applicable. Forecast/model evidence and observed/official evidence must remain distinguishable.

## Navigation gate

For the ordinary consumer, the target primary structure is:

- My Tomorrow
- Protected
- Explore
- You

Existing Dashboard, My Area, Outlook, Predict, Action Center, Alerts, Safe Route, Evidence and Reports remain capabilities until their jobs are proven to be fully absorbed. They are not deleted merely to make the navigation look simpler.

The navigation is collapsed only after the new surfaces can reproduce the old jobs without hiding evidence, action or safety functionality.

## Release test

Before the consumer redesign can replace the current navigation, reviewers must be able to answer YES to all of these:

- Does a new visitor get useful value without creating an account?
- Can an existing user understand tomorrow in under one screenful?
- Can the user see Safety, Money and Time consequences without technical language?
- Is there a clear action when action is warranted?
- Is a quiet day useful without fake urgency?
- Can the user share useful intelligence directly?
- Is the reason to save people/places/assets obvious?
- Is the reason to return tomorrow obvious?
- Is the reason to pay for deeper protection/planning understandable after free value is demonstrated?
- Are safety-critical warnings still free?
- Are evidence, provenance and official-warning precedence preserved?
- Are confidence and naira figures honest and supportable?
- Does the new journey preserve every important capability of the old build or provide a documented replacement?

If any answer is NO, the new consumer experience has not yet beaten the original build and must remain a prototype.
