# My Tomorrow User Journey v1

Status: FROZEN JOURNEY BEFORE FEATURE BUILD-OUT

## The promise

NaijaClimaGuard tells you what tomorrow could take from you before it gets the chance.

The user should feel that promise before learning anything about models, sources, dashboards or subscriptions.

## First visit

### Entry

A person can arrive from the homepage, a shared warning, a search result, a QR link or a direct My Tomorrow link.

The first useful check does not require an account.

The page asks for current location or a place the person cares about. A shared link may arrive with the place already selected.

### First answer

The first answer is one briefing, not a dashboard tour.

It answers in this order:

1. What is coming or building?
2. When could it matter?
3. Safety: what could put the person or people they care about at risk?
4. Money: what kind of exposure or avoidable cost may exist, without inventing a naira figure?
5. Time: what movement, route or timing could be affected?
6. Protect: what should be moved, watched, changed or prepared?
7. Do this: the most useful immediate action.
8. Why: evidence, source freshness, official-warning context and model limitations.

A quiet answer is equally valid: nothing serious is developing and no special action is needed.

## The first conversion is not payment

After the person receives value, the first conversion is:

**Protect this place.**

The user is invited to save it as Home, Work, Shop, School, Family, Farm or another useful label.

Creating an account becomes useful here because the system needs somewhere to remember the protected place and send future changes. Registration is therefore attached to a clear benefit rather than blocking the first check.

## The return loop

On later visits My Tomorrow opens with protected places already understood.

The briefing should answer both current state and change:

- what changed since the last check;
- which protected place changed;
- whether the timing moved earlier/later;
- whether severity increased/decreased;
- whether a new action is warranted;
- whether an earlier concern has cleared.

The reason to return is changing intelligence about things the person cares about. There are no meaningless streaks or manufactured emergencies.

## Quiet-day journey

Quiet days build trust.

The product says clearly that nothing serious is developing, then gives only useful planning context if it exists. It should not turn ordinary weather into danger for engagement.

A quiet day can still show:

- protected places all clear;
- normal route clear;
- no special protection action;
- next meaningful check time if conditions are expected to change;
- last meaningful change.

## Developing-risk journey

When a signal is developing, the briefing becomes progressively more actionable.

Watch means monitor and prepare, not panic.

Elevated means a credible disruption may require preparation.

High means the strongest supported action language, while official emergency instructions retain precedence where applicable.

The product must distinguish forecast/model intelligence from official/observed warnings.

## The share loop

The user shares intelligence, not an invitation.

Primary share intents:

- Send this warning to someone there.
- Send this to family.
- Share this route/area update.
- Share that this place is currently clear when reassurance itself is useful.

The shared object contains:

- place;
- valid time;
- current hazard/status;
- plain-language consequence;
- recommended action where applicable;
- generated/updated time;
- evidence/source context;
- a link that opens the same place in My Tomorrow.

The recipient sees the useful briefing before signup. After receiving value they can protect the place themselves.

## The protection loop

Protected is the ongoing monitoring layer.

Free protection should support the core safety use case. Premium should deepen coverage and coordination rather than withholding urgent safety information.

Potential protected objects include:

- Home
- Work
- Shop/business
- School
- Family member's place
- Farm
- Important route
- Later, supported assets where exposure data is trustworthy

The user sees why each object is being watched and what changed.

## The payment moment

Do not present a generic upgrade wall immediately after the first weather check.

The paid proposition appears when the user has already experienced the value of protection and wants more of it.

Examples of legitimate premium demand:

- protect more people/places/assets;
- coordinate a household/family protection circle;
- longer planning horizon where evidence supports it;
- deeper change history and evidence history;
- advanced route monitoring;
- richer asset/business exposure analysis;
- validated economic exposure ranges;
- downloadable personal/property risk history where appropriate;
- more granular notification controls.

The language is protection and planning, not fear.

Safety-critical warnings, official-warning context and the first useful local briefing remain free.

## Suggested plan logic for testing

### Free

Purpose: become trusted and useful.

Includes first local check, core My Tomorrow briefing, essential warnings, official-warning context, limited protected places, simple actions, evidence summary and contextual sharing.

### Plus / Personal Protection

Purpose: protect more of the person's life and reduce repeated planning work.

Candidate value: more protected places/people/routes, richer change history, coordination, advanced route monitoring, deeper planning and supported exposure insights.

No final price is frozen in this journey document. Pricing must be tested against Nigerian willingness to pay and product cost rather than invented for a pitch slide.

## Notification philosophy

Notify only when the information is materially useful.

Examples:

- a protected place moves into a meaningful risk state;
- timing changes enough to affect a plan;
- severity materially rises;
- an official warning becomes active;
- a recommended action changes;
- risk clears after an earlier warning.

Do not notify simply because another forecast refresh happened.

## Multi-hazard behavior

The user does not manage separate flood, heat, storm and drought apps inside NaijaClimaGuard.

My Tomorrow ranks the relevant hazards and tells the user what matters most. Additional hazards sit below the primary answer.

A new hazard is allowed into the consumer experience only when its source, interpretation, validation and user action are good enough to be useful.

## Outcome loop

After a warned event, the product should eventually ask lightweight, optional questions that improve truth without burdening the user:

- Did this happen around you?
- Was this road affected?
- Did you take the suggested action?

These observations must carry verification/provenance status. They are not automatically treated as ground truth or automatically used to retrain a model.

When evidence supports it, the app can later show whether an action plausibly reduced exposure. It must not fabricate money saved.

## Enterprise flywheel boundary

Consumer activity can help build aggregated climate-impact intelligence, but personal identity is not the product sold to institutions.

The valuable enterprise layer is aggregated evidence about how hazards affect places, movement, operations, demand, disruption and loss patterns.

Consent, minimisation, aggregation and privacy controls must be designed before personal observations are used beyond the person's own service.

## Journey success tests

The journey is successful only when a person can:

- get value before signup;
- understand the main answer quickly;
- know what to do when action is necessary;
- trust a quiet day;
- protect a place because the benefit is obvious;
- understand what changed on return;
- share something useful without becoming an advertiser;
- understand why deeper paid protection could be worth paying for;
- reach evidence when they want proof;
- never be forced to pay for urgent safety information.
